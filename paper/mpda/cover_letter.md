Dear Editor,

We submit "CTSpinoPelvic1K: spine, pelvis, ribs and femora in one coordinate frame, annotated for lumbosacral transitional anatomy" for consideration as a Dataset and Software Article in Medical Physics.

The article describes an open dataset of 802 abdominal CT records from a single prospective screening protocol (ACRIN 6664), with radiologist-sourced vertebral and pelvic annotation placed on one coordinate frame, per-level ribs and femora, a label scheme that can record a sixth lumbar vertebra, a lumbar rib, a separate first sacral segment and surgical instrumentation, two-reader consensus Castellvi grades, and layered validation. The dataset is archived on Zenodo under a persistent identifier (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22139642; the version described is 10.5281/zenodo.22642578) and is publicly available without restriction, together with the code, the label-scheme definition, the quality-control tables and the pseudolabelling checkpoints.

The manuscript is submitted in the review format the author guidelines ask for (single column, 1.5 spacing, line and page numbers). In the journal two-column layout, checked with the REVTeX aapm reprint style, it typesets to ten pages, the limit for a dataset article, with seven figures and three tables. The manuscript is prepared for double-anonymized review. Author names, affiliations, contributions, conflicts of interest and acknowledgements are on the separate title page, and the consortium name, one self-citation and the code and model repository URLs are redacted in the manuscript. PUB 3-C requires the dataset DOI to be cited, and the dataset is a public release under its own name: the Zenodo record the DOI resolves to, the code and model repositories, and a published description of our student research program that names the dataset all identify the authors to a reader who follows them. We disclose this rather than obscure it. No preprint of this manuscript has been posted; we intend to post the submitted version to arXiv, a non-commercial server, immediately after submission, and will link it to the article DOI on acceptance. The manuscript is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have read and approved the submission; there are no conflicts of interest and no funding to declare. The work uses publicly available de-identified imaging; the institutional review board determined it is not human participant research (determination on the title page).

Thank you for considering it.
