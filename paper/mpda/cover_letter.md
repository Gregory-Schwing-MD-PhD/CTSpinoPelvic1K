Dear Editor,

We submit "CTSpinoPelvic1K: spine, pelvis, ribs and femora in one coordinate frame, annotated for lumbosacral transitional anatomy" for consideration as a Dataset and Software Article in Medical Physics.

The article describes an open dataset of 802 abdominal CT records from a single prospective screening protocol (ACRIN 6664), with radiologist-sourced vertebral and pelvic annotation placed on one coordinate frame, per-level ribs and femora, a label scheme that can record a sixth lumbar vertebra, a thirteenth thoracic vertebra, a lumbar rib and surgical instrumentation, two-reader consensus Castellvi grades, and layered validation. The dataset is archived on Zenodo under a persistent identifier (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22139642; the version described, v11, is 10.5281/zenodo.22797014, published 18 September 2026) and is publicly available under a Creative Commons licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), with no restriction on scientific use, together with the label-scheme definition and the quality-control tables. The code that built and measured it, the measurement toolkit and the pseudolabelling checkpoints are in public repositories, listed on the title page.

This resubmission answers the editorial office's check of the supporting information. The six supporting items are now supplied as one document, Supporting Information, containing Figure S1, Figure S2, Figure S3, Table S1, Table S2 and Table S3 in that order, numbered as the text cites them; the main document carries no supplementary figure, table or caption, only a one-sentence pointer to that document. As in the previous resubmission, the main document carries no abstract; the structured abstract is entered in the submission form.

The manuscript is submitted in review format (single column, double spaced, line and page numbers). In the journal two-column layout, checked with the REVTeX aapm reprint style, it typesets to ten pages including the abstract, the limit for a dataset article, with three figures and three tables; the three further figures and three tables are the supporting information. As PUB 3-C requires, the analysis is descriptive and reports no tests of statistical significance; we have answered the AI checklist item on significance testing accordingly. The manuscript is prepared for double-anonymized review. Author names, affiliations, contributions, conflicts of interest and acknowledgements are on the separate title page, and the consortium name, two self-citations and the code and model repository URLs are redacted in the manuscript. We disclose this rather than obscure it.

It is cited in the manuscript, redacted in the review copy, and shares no text, figure or table with it.
