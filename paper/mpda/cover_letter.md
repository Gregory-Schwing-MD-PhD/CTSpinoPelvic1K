Dear Editor,

We submit "CTSpinoPelvic1K: spine, pelvis, ribs and femora in one coordinate frame, annotated for lumbosacral transitional anatomy" for consideration as a Dataset and Software Article in Medical Physics.

The article describes an open dataset of 802 abdominal CT records from a single prospective screening protocol (ACRIN 6664), with radiologist-sourced vertebral and pelvic annotation placed on one coordinate frame, per-level ribs and femora, a label scheme that can record a sixth lumbar vertebra, a thirteenth thoracic vertebra, a lumbar rib and surgical instrumentation, two-reader consensus Castellvi grades, and layered validation. The dataset is archived on Zenodo under a persistent identifier (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22139642; the version described, v11, is 10.5281/zenodo.22797014) and is publicly available without restriction, together with the code, the label-scheme definition, the quality-control tables and the pseudolabelling checkpoints.

This resubmission removes the abstract from the main document, as the editorial office asked; the structured abstract is entered in the submission form. The manuscript is submitted in review format (single column, double spaced, line and page numbers). In the journal two-column layout, checked with the REVTeX aapm reprint style, it typesets to ten pages including the abstract, the limit for a dataset article, with three figures and three tables; three further figures and three tables are supporting information. As PUB 3-C requires, the analysis is descriptive and reports no tests of statistical significance; we have answered the AI checklist item on significance testing accordingly. The manuscript is prepared for double-anonymized review. Author names, affiliations, contributions, conflicts of interest and acknowledgements are on the separate title page, and the consortium name, one self-citation and the code and model repository URLs are redacted in the manuscript. PUB 3-C requires the dataset DOI to be cited, and the dataset is a public release under its own name: the Zenodo record the DOI resolves to, the code and model repositories, and a published description of our student research program that names the dataset all identify the authors to a reader who follows them. We disclose this rather than obscure it. A preprint of the submitted version is posted on arXiv, a non-commercial server (arXiv:2609.22760, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22760), and will be linked to the article DOI on acceptance. The manuscript is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have read and approved the submission; there are no conflicts of interest and no funding to declare. The work uses publicly available de-identified imaging; the institutional review board determined it is not human participant research (determination on the title page).

Thank you for considering it.
