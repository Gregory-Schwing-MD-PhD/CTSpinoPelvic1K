Dear Editor,

We submit "CTSpinoPelvic1K: spine, pelvis, ribs and femora in one coordinate frame, annotated for lumbosacral transitional anatomy" for consideration as a Dataset and Software Article in Medical Physics.

The article describes an open dataset of 802 abdominal CT records from a single prospective screening protocol (ACRIN 6664), with radiologist-sourced vertebral and pelvic annotation placed on one coordinate frame, per-level ribs and femora, a label scheme that can record a sixth lumbar vertebra, a lumbar rib, a separate first sacral segment and surgical instrumentation, two-reader consensus Castellvi grades, and layered validation. The dataset is archived on Zenodo under a persistent identifier (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22139642; the version described is 10.5281/zenodo.22293878) and is publicly available without restriction, together with the code, the label-scheme definition, the quality-control tables and the pseudolabelling checkpoints.

The manuscript is prepared for double-anonymized review: author names, affiliations, contributions, conflicts of interest and acknowledgements are on the separate title page, and the consortium name and one self-citation are redacted in the manuscript. It has not been published or submitted elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the submission; there are no conflicts of interest and no funding to declare. The work uses publicly available de-identified imaging; the institutional review board determined it is not human participant research (determination on the title page).

Thank you for considering it.
