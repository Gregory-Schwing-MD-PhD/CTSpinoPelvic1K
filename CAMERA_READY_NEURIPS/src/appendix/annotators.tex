\section{Annotators}
\label{app:annotators}

The vertebra, sacrum, hip and rib labels released with \dataset{} were corrected and reviewed by eleven
medical students at Wayne State University School of Medicine over 544 case assignments, recorded in the
project's review ledger and reproduced in Table~\ref{tab:annotators}. Spine assignments cover the per-level
vertebrae, sacrum and hips; rib assignments cover the per-level rib labels. A case may be assigned to more
than one reviewer, so the assignment count exceeds the number of distinct cases.

\paragraph{Coordination.}
Ashley Schehr carried the largest share, 142 assignments, a quarter of the student review and almost twice
the next largest, and she organised the effort as well as working in it. The project's correspondence
records her forming the first student group in May 2026 and writing on its behalf, the group undertaking
the spine-pathology and annotation work, and her arranging the meetings at which it was divided. She was
the first of the students onto the university computing cluster and then directed others to it, advised
them on running their analyses at the scale of the full release, and ran other students' code over all
1{,}153 records of the April release on their behalf.

\paragraph{What is hers, and what is not in this paper.}
Two measures derived from this release are hers: an automated Cobb angle with a scoliosis-threshold
assignment, and per-vertebra attenuation in Hounsfield units. \emph{Neither is a result of this paper}, and
neither is used anywhere in it; they belong to the companion dataset article and to her own first-author
work on lateral interbody fusion planning. Her contribution here is to the labels this paper releases and
evaluates against, to the organisation of the review that produced them, and to the literature sources
behind Appendix~\ref{app:background}.
